# Testing phase

Working copy for the founder. Use this until Google Drive is connected, then copy it into **Pedra e Promessa / Business**. This is a plan of work already decided. It is not legal, tax or insurance advice. A lawyer reviews drafts at the end. An accountant confirms the tax points marked below.

## What this phase is

Pedra e Promessa starts inside the existing Dutch ZZP. The main job moves out of the ZZP into its own company. That company, and the holding around it, are separate from Pedra e Promessa. The ZZP stays open only for this testing phase.

Monthly deals wait for a Dutch BV. An immediate-payment deal may happen in the ZZP once insurance is in place.

Losses in the ZZP are meant to offset the founder's other Box 1 income. Confirm that treatment with the accountant. The recorded reason for separate books is this offset, plus a clean record when the BV is created.

## Do now

1. Keep the ZZP open. Do not fold Pedra e Promessa into the main-job company.
2. Open a bank account used only for Pedra e Promessa income and costs. Pay Pedra costs from it and receive Pedra income into it.
3. Keep separate bookkeeping for that account from the first movement. No salaries and no co-founders.
4. Ask the accountant to confirm two things before any BV work for Pedra: that ZZP losses offset the founder's other income as planned, and that a low customary salary (*gebruikelijk loon*, €58,000 in 2026) would be accepted for a Pedra BV. Do not set up the Pedra BV until that answer is in.
5. Arrange insurance before the first immediate-payment deal. Do not sign that deal in the ZZP without it. The policy itself is still to be chosen with the insurer.
6. Use `Sales/leads.csv` for every introduction. Use `Sales/one-pager.html` and `Sales/intro-message.md` when a contact makes an introduction.

## Do not do in this phase

- A monthly deal. Those happen only in the BV.
- Register with IMPIC. That comes with the BV, under Lei 15/2013 article 21.
- Order an independent valuation before the seller has signed the mediation contract. The company pays that valuation. The company does not value homes it mediates.
- Tell a contact the name of a buyer before the mediation contract is signed.
- Let a contact present an offer, discuss price, collect documents or negotiate. They introduce a possible seller and stop there.
- Let the founder's parents, spouse or children buy a home the company mediates.
- Advertise the company's fee. Each party is shown only their own figure. The fee sits in the service contract.
- Put business money into personal accounts. The separate Pedra account is the record.

The contract drafts and the older setup guide in Drive still say "Unipessoal, Lda." Leave them until they can be read. When the BV exists, replace that name. Do not start a second set of contracts from memory.

## Before each trip to Portugal

The founder is in Portugal about 8 weeks a year, in 3 or 4 trips of 2 to 3 weeks.

Before the trip, from the Netherlands: introductions, calls, video meetings with families, draft contracts, and line up valuations for sellers who have already signed.

On the trip: home visits, proposals and signatures.

## When the BV is due

Set up the Pedra BV before the first monthly deal, or when 2 or 3 sellers are serious, and only after the accountant has answered on the customary salary.

If the holding BV does not already exist by then, create the holding at the same time and let the holding found the Pedra BV. The target is a holding with an operating company for the boats work and a Pedra e Promessa BV under it.

Then register the Pedra BV in Portugal with IMPIC under Lei 15/2013 article 21, and update every draft that still says "Unipessoal, Lda."
