# Pedra e Promessa — overview

Working copy. When Google Drive is connected, copy this into **Pedra e Promessa / Business** and keep that Drive doc in step with decisions recorded in `CLAUDE.md`.

Pedra e Promessa connects generations. An older homeowner sells the home to a younger buyer, keeps the right to live there for life, and receives either a monthly payment or the price at once. The company is a mediator. It never becomes the owner.

The founder works alone, with personal contacts and a lawyer. No salaries. No co-founders. The founder lives in the Netherlands and is in Portugal about 8 weeks a year.

## The two deals

Over the seller's expected remaining life (age and sex, INE life tables), the buyer pays about half the home's value. The monthly amount stops rising after age 78: an older seller receives the same monthly amount as a 78-year-old. The reference monthly payment in the model is €400.

| Deal | What the seller pays the company | When it can be done |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | €1,500 setup, plus 10% of each payment, plus 23% VAT on the fee | Only in the Dutch BV |
| Immediate | 5% of the sale price, plus 23% VAT on the fee | In the ZZP during testing, with insurance, or later in the BV |

On the €400 reference payment the seller receives €350.80. The buyer is shown what they pay. The seller is shown what they receive. The fee is stated in the service contract and is not advertised.

All fees are charged to the seller only. Mediation law forbids fees from both sides.

## If the buyer stops paying

If the buyer misses 5 monthly payments, or dies and the heirs do not choose within 90 days to keep paying, the home returns to the seller. It never returns to the company or the founder.

On return, the seller refunds 80% of the rent received in the last 12 months, in up to 12 monthly instalments, or at once from a new sale if one happens first. Everything else stays with the seller.

## How a deal is protected

The seller signs a mediation contract before any buyer is named. The fee is due for any sale to a buyer the company introduced in writing, during the contract or within 12 months after it. The 10% monthly share is part of that fee, paid in instalments, so stopping the payment service does not stop the fee. The buyer signs a declaration that the company introduced them.

Contacts only introduce a possible seller. They do not present offers, discuss prices, collect documents or negotiate. The founder's parents, spouse and children never buy a home the company mediates.

## Who pays which cost

The company pays its own lawyer and the independent valuation. The valuation is ordered only after the mediation contract is signed. The seller's lawyer is recommended and paid by the seller. The buyer pays the notary, the registry and the purchase taxes.

While the seller holds the usufruct they keep paying IMI. The buyer pays IMT and 0.8% stamp duty on the larger of the legal *nua propriedade* value and the contract value.

## Company, in the order already decided

1. Now: a holding BV and a separate company for the main job. The main job leaves the ZZP. Pedra e Promessa is not part of that company.
2. Testing: Pedra e Promessa inside the ZZP, with its own bank account and bookkeeping. See `testing-phase.md`.
3. Before the first monthly deal, or with 2–3 serious sellers: a Dutch BV for Pedra e Promessa, founded by the holding if the holding is created at the same time, then registered in Portugal with IMPIC under Lei 15/2013 article 21. Only after the accountant accepts a low customary salary (€58,000 in 2026).
4. Later, if the work expands to other countries or the boats company needs the holding: holding BV, boats BV, and Pedra e Promessa BV. Pedra profits are taxed at 19% inside that structure.

The founder wants business money kept out of personal assets. The founder drafts documents. A lawyer reviews them at the end.
